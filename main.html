Kesari

Actors Favorite Quote
Kesari is a historical war film based on the Battle of Saragarhi. The movie tells the inspiring story of 21 Sikh soldiers who bravely fought against thousands of invaders to defend their post.
Main actors include Akshay Kumar, Parineeti Chopra, Vansh Bhardwaj, and Vikram Kochhar.
